01 · The computer

It starts in your pocket.

Inside the Termux app, your phone runs Ubuntu with a full Linux desktop.

02 · The screen

Vox sends it to your glasses.

A second desktop, sized for the lens: 640 × 480, over Bluetooth LE.

03 · The keyboard

Type, and it lands in your glasses.

Any Bluetooth keyboard paired to your phone types straight into it.

04 · The tabs

Your tabs sit around you.

Turn your head, and the tab you face comes up.

05 · The apps

The web. Your files. Your code.

If Linux or Android runs it, your glasses can show it.
