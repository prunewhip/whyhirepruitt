Even Suite

Private when it can be. Powerful when you need it.

The AI that fits on a phone is private, but small, and small models make more mistakes. The most capable AI lives in the cloud. Getting the most out of AI means choosing, question by question.

Vox Amelior keeps your everyday conversations on your phone. When a question needs the best answer, Even Suite puts Claude in your glasses, and only when you ask: say “Claude”, hold the TouchBar or type. The reply appears in your view, five lines a page, and listening turns itself off.

  1. On your phonePrivate, always with you, good for everyday memory. Vox Amelior.
  2. In the cloudThe most capable model, for when accuracy matters. Even Suite with Claude.
  3. On your computer NextNot built yet: Vox Amelior on your own computer, with a larger model behind the glasses. More accuracy than a phone, and your words still stay at home.
  • Wake word
  • Glasses microphone
  • Claude Code bridge
  • 87 tests against simulated glasses
  • No API keys. The app connects only to your glasses and to Claude Code on your phone

03 · Speaks to you · Android

Narrator Studio

What you hear shapes how you think. So choose it.

How much of what reaches your ears did you actually pick? Much of it comes from a feed built to keep you listening. Modern AI voices make it possible to choose instead.

Narrator Studio turns the article you saved, the paper you meant to read, a chapter of a book or your own notes into a podcast with chapters, read in natural voices made on your phone. Four voice engines run on the device, and voice cloning works only with consent.

Pick the words. Pick the voice. Press play.

Listen: “Why Is the Sky Blue?”
