01 · The idea

Three ways in. You stay in charge.

Each app is one sense of the same interface: it hears you, it shows you, it speaks to you. And each one hands back a choice the usual apps make for you.

  1. 01 · Hears you

    Vox Amelior

    Listens and remembers on your phone, then helps you hear yourself. Your conversations stay on the phone.

02 · The main app · Vox Amelior · Android

A private memory for your conversations.

It writes down what’s said nearby, learns who’s speaking and answers when you ask. Then it does something a feed never will: it helps you hear yourself.

Remembers
Speech to text and speaker recognition, on the phone (NVIDIA Parakeet, TitaNet).
Answers
Ask in plain words; a local language model and search by meaning find it.
Reviews
Promises, kind words, logical fallacies, or any question you ask about your own words.
Stays yours
No cloud AI and no analytics. Private storage under Android’s encryption, kept out of cloud backups.
  • 400+ automated tests
  • Release build tested on an emulator with the real models
