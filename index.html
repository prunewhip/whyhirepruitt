02 · New · Vox · Android + Even Realities G1

A second screen for every AI.

Smart glasses usually come locked to one maker’s apps. ScouterKit, open-source firmware by Marek, unlocks the G1’s whole 640 × 480 display at up to 30 frames a second. On top of it I built Vox, the Android app that makes those lenses a screen any AI can use.

Any terminal AI
Claude Code in your glasses, on the phone or a laptop. Any AI with a command line works the same way.
Any app
Your phone’s screen, or your laptop’s, mirrored and tuned to read well on the lenses.
Your voice
Speak into the glasses’ microphone; NVIDIA Parakeet, on the phone, types it into any prompt.
The room
Live captions and an all-day transcript that knows who’s speaking, carried over from Vox Amelior.
On the road
Ask Claude from the steering wheel. The answer is read aloud; the lenses stay dark while you drive.
At a glance
Look up for the time, the weather, your next event and the notifications that matter.

If it runs on your phone or in a terminal, it can be in your glasses. The next AI needs no new app to get there.

How I build

Find it. Fit it. Ship it.

Better open software ships every month. The work is knowing which pieces win, and making them run together on a phone you carry all day.

  1. 01 · Find

    I test the candidates on a real phone and keep what wins: Parakeet for speech, Liquid’s LFM2.5 for thinking on the phone, Kokoro for voices.

  2. 02 · Fit

    Then I make them share one phone: one microphone for every feature, a queue on disk so nothing said is lost, models that take turns instead of fighting for the chip.

  3. 03 · Ship

    Every change builds in the open and runs its tests, and the result goes on the phone and glasses I use every day.

03 · Where it started · Vox Amelior · Android

A private memory for your conversations.

It writes down what’s said nearby, learns who’s speaking and answers when you ask. Then it does something a feed never will: it helps you hear yourself. Its listening is what Vox now brings to the glasses.

Remembers
Speech to text and speaker recognition, on the phone (NVIDIA Parakeet, TitaNet).
Answers
Ask in plain words; a local language model and search by meaning find it.
Reviews
Promises, kind words, logical fallacies, or any question you ask about your own words.
Stays yours
No cloud AI and no analytics. Private storage under Android’s encryption, kept out of cloud backups.
  • 400+ automated tests
  • Release build tested on an emulator with the real models
