About me

From building networks to AI that stays home.

I’m an AI engineer. I build speech, voice and language apps for Android, three of them public. Here’s how a network engineer got here, in 87 seconds.

My story

For seven years I built the networks our words travel on. At SubCom I worked on subsea cable systems, at Wyyerd Fiber I troubleshot the fiber that brings internet into homes, and at BTI Solutions, as a network engineer, I validated 5G and LTE sites for Tier-1 carriers. I worked my way up from technician to engineer, and in every job I fixed the process as well as the problem: a fiber inspection workflow that took half the time, a troubleshooting knowledge base that saved more than $100K a year, test scripts rolled out to every workstation.

Then the first GPT models arrived, and a computer wrote back. I was ecstatic, and I still am. I’ve tried and reviewed every major model since.

Years in networking leave you with one reflex: ask where the data goes. Most of what we type into AI apps leaves the house, on roads like the ones I helped build. So I built Vox Amelior, an AI that listens, remembers and answers on your phone, and works in airplane mode. Two more apps followed: Even Suite puts Claude in smart glasses when you want the most capable model, and Narrator Studio reads to you in voices made on your phone.

Along the way I earned a Master’s in Software Engineering with an AI focus. Now I’m looking for a full-time team to build what’s next with.

What I work with

On-device AI
Speech recognition and speaker ID (NVIDIA Parakeet, TitaNet, Sortformer), text to speech and voice cloning (Kokoro, Supertonic, Pocket TTS, VibeVoice), on-device LLMs (Gemma), embeddings and hybrid search, ONNX conversion and quantization
Mobile
Flutter and Dart, Kotlin, native code through FFI (sherpa-onnx, ONNX Runtime, LiteRT-LM), Android background services and audio, SQLite, Bluetooth Low Energy
Testing & delivery
Unit, widget and integration tests, emulator runs with real models in CI, Whisper-based audio checks, GitHub Actions, signed releases
Backend
Python, FastAPI, Redis, PostgreSQL, Docker, REST and WebSocket APIs

Selected work

3 public apps

AI that runs on the device

  • Vox Amelior: an always-on Android assistant with on-device speech recognition, speaker ID, search by meaning and a local LLM; 400+ automated tests.
  • Narrator Studio: text to podcast with four on-device voice engines, chaptered export and consent-gated voice cloning. A sample episode is checked by Whisper on every build: 2.6% word error rate.
  • Even Suite: a hands-free Claude assistant on Even Realities G1 glasses; 87 tests run against simulated glasses.
  • Earlier, Vox Server: a self-hosted, multi-user prototype built with FastAPI services and Docker, where speech and the language model take turns on one GPU.
